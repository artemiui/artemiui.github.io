The official University of the Philippines website featured the Philippine Junior Data Science Challenge (PJDSC) 2026 Project Development Workshop. I'm the project manager of PJDSC 2026, and I'm sharing the feature here as part of my work with UP Data Science Society.

## The workshop

Held on October 17, 2026 at the College of Science Auditorium, UP Diliman, the workshop was organized by UP Data Science Society and the UP Intelligent Systems Center to help participants develop projects addressing public health challenges.

Dr. Paulyn Jean Claro discussed vaccination coverage and the value of understanding the social and behavioral factors behind health data. Uriel Alonso guided participants in turning broad problems into feasible research questions. Assistant Professor Philip Christian Zuñiga discussed data quality, inclusion, and the consequences of leaving marginalized communities out of datasets. Participants also practiced communicating data to different stakeholders.
